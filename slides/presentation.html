Market Saturation

Saturated Market Dynamics (GSMA 2025)

In mature telecommunications ecosystems, organic market expansion is virtually non-existent. Growth is zero-sum: carriers gain subscribers only by winning them from competitors or cross-selling existing accounts.

The Handset Replacement Trigger

Subscribers do not leave on a whim. Attrition occurs at contractual and physical inflection points — primarily when equipment reaches maturity (18–24 months) and competitive trade-in offers trigger a switch.

Key Market Insight #1

Protecting ARPU is paramount: A 5% reduction in customer churn increases operator operating profits by 25% to 85% (Analysys Mason, 2024).

Key Market Insight #2

Device Financing Cliff: When handset financing concludes, customers experience a drop in switching friction unless actively re-contracted with next-gen hardware.

Key Market Insight #3

5G Migration Opportunity: Transitioning subscribers to 5G-capable devices drives a proven +12% lift in data usage and ARPU expansion.

Empirical Discovery

The #1 Actionable Trigger: Handset Age Accelerates Churn by +12.7%

Operational Root Cause

The Hardware Wear Effect

Across ~100 features tested, `eqpdays` (handset age in days) emerges as the single most powerful driver of customer disengagement:

  • Handsets < 1 Year: Churn rate baseline of 45.19%
  • Handsets 1–2 Years: Churn increases to 53.85% (+8.7 pts)
  • Handsets > 2 Years: Churn surges to 57.89% (+12.7 pts)

Actionability vs. Immutability

A telecommunications operator cannot change a customer's credit score, dwelling size, or geography. However, an operator has complete control over handset upgrade subsidies. Hardware replacement is the prime actionable intervention lever.

Root Cause Deep Dive

Secondary Catalysts: Network Drop Friction & "Bill Shock"

Behavioral Signals

1. Network Quality Degradation

Dropped and blocked calls (`drop_vce_Mean`, `blck_vce_Mean`) show an exponential relationship with customer support escalations (`custcare_Mean`).

Subscribers experiencing > 5 dropped calls/month are 1.8x more likely to churn, especially when paired with aging 3G/4G devices lacking modern carrier aggregation.

2. Bill Shock & Overage Friction

Data and voice overage charges (`ovrrev_Mean`, `datovr_Mean`) create intense consumer resentment. A sudden monthly billing spike of >$20 precedes churn events in 31% of cases.

The Friction Cascade Model

Step 1: Physical Friction
Battery life deteriorates, older antennas drop calls in dense areas (`eqpdays > 365`).
Step 2: Billing Friction
Data needs exceed plan limits; customer is hit with surprise overage charges (`ovrrev > 0`).
Step 3: Escalation & Defection
Customer calls support (`custcare > 2`), competitor offers free 5G device trade-in -> Churn.

Risk Management

Risk Mitigation & Program Governance

Board Assurance

Risk 1: Cannibalization & Deadweight Loss

Threat: Giving subsidized phones to subscribers who would have stayed anyway without incentive.

Mitigation: Strict exclusion cutoff: only subscribers with predicted churn probability $P(\text{churn}) > 0.55$ AND $eqpdays > 365$ receive the offer.

Risk 2: Hardware Supply Chain Constraints

Threat: Bulk device shortages delaying fulfillment.

Mitigation: Dual-vendor OEM partnership (e.g., Samsung Galaxy A-series & Google Pixel) guaranteeing volume delivery within 5 business days.

Risk 3: Model Degradation & Feature Drift

Threat: Shifting customer usage patterns post-campaign eroding model predictive power.

Mitigation: Continuous Population Stability Index (PSI) monitoring with automated retraining trigger if PSI exceeds 0.25.

Risk 4: Regulatory & Fair Lending Compliance

Threat: Accusations of discriminatory credit redlining.

Mitigation: All demographic features (`ethnic`, `marital`, `adults`) strictly excluded from feature weighting. Scoring is 100% behavioral.
