Market Penetration

Zero-Sum Market Saturation

In mature telecommunications economies, user growth is strictly zero-sum. Carriers cannot rely on organic adoption; every single customer gained is captured from a rival, making subscriber defense the primary driver of corporate margin.

The Handset Replacement Pivot

Subscribers rarely churn purely over minor network quirks. Departure is triggered when physical equipment ages (18–24 months), coinciding with the end of device amortization and competitive trade-in promotions.

Core Economic Law

The 5% Retention Multiplier

A 5% reduction in customer churn accelerates operator operating profits by 25% to 85% due to long-tail customer lifetime value (Analysys Mason, 2024).

Structural Inflection

The Device Financing Cliff

When device financing reaches term, psychological customer inertia drops to zero. Proactive re-contracting is mandatory before the customer seeks outside deals.

Network Modernization

The 5G ARPU Uplift

Upgrading subscribers to modern 5G handsets delivers a documented +12% lift in monthly data usage, stabilizing long-term recurring revenue.

Empirical Discovery

The Handset Aging Effect: Churn Surges by +12.7%

Root Cause Isolation

The Hardware Wear Trigger

Across the complete 100-variable feature space, `eqpdays` (handset age in days) emerges as the decisive physical catalyst of attrition:

  • Handset < 1 Year: Baseline churn of 45.19%
  • Handset 1–2 Years: Churn climbs to 53.85% (+8.66 pts)
  • Handset > 2 Years: Churn spikes to 57.89% (+12.7 pts)

Actionable vs. Structural Features

An operator cannot alter a customer's demographics or living location. However, hardware subsidization is 100% within management control. Device refresh represents the ideal commercial lever.

Behavioral Mechanics

Secondary Friction: Network Drops & “Bill Shock”

Defection Signals

1. Network Quality Failure Points

Dropped and blocked calls (`drop_vce_Mean`, `blck_vce_Mean`) correlate strongly with support escalations (`custcare_Mean`).

Subscribers encountering > 5 dropped calls/month are 1.8x more likely to churn, primarily due to older antennas lacking carrier aggregation.

2. Bill Shock & Overage Friction

Data and voice overage charges (`ovrrev_Mean`, `datovr_Mean`) trigger sharp customer dissatisfaction. Unanticipated billing spikes above $20 precede churn events in 31% of cases.

The Friction Cascade Pathway

1. Physical Wear: Battery fatigue, slower processor, legacy 4G antenna drops calls in dense coverage areas (`eqpdays > 365`).
2. Billing Spike: Modern apps demand higher bandwidth; user exceeds plan limits and receives surprise overage charges (`ovrrev > 0`).
3. Competitor Switch: Customer contacts support (`custcare > 2`); competitor offers modern 5G trade-in → Permanent Churn.

Methodology

Production ML Architecture: 5-Fold Stratified Validation

Data Science Rigor

Data Engineering & Hygiene

  • Consolidation: 100% 1:1 join across 100,000 records from `Client.csv` and `Record.csv`.
  • Memory Footprint: Float32 typed matrix (41.2 MB RAM) with zero memory fragmentation.
  • Feature Engineering: 8 domain behavioral ratios (Depreciation index, network failure rate, overage intensity).
  • Leakage Prevention: Out-of-Fold target encoding; strictly zero lookahead bias.

Cross-Validation Architecture

To eliminate overfitting, all metrics and probabilities are derived Out-Of-Fold across 5 independent stratified splits (20,000 validation records per fold).

End-to-End Pipeline

1. Ingestion: 100k records • 100 raw features

2. Feature Transformation: Categorical encoding • Domain ratios
  ↓

  • Baseline: Logistic Regression (L2 scaled)

  • Production: LightGBM GBDT (Optimized)
  ↓
4. Probability Calibration: Brier Score verification
  ↓
5. Value Scoring: Expected Loss = P(churn) × ARPU

Governance

Comprehensive Risk Mitigation Framework

Board Assurance

Risk 1: Cannibalization & Deadweight Subsidy

Threat: Offering subsidized handsets to subscribers who would have remained without incentive.

Mitigation: Strict dual-gated qualification: only accounts with calibrated defection probability > 0.55 AND handset age > 365 days are eligible.

Risk 2: Supply Chain Fulfillment Latency

Threat: OEM hardware shortages delaying device delivery.

Mitigation: Dual-vendor procurement framework (Samsung Galaxy A-series & Google Pixel) guaranteeing 5-day delivery SLAs.

Risk 3: Model Drift & Feature Degradation

Threat: Post-campaign consumer behavioral shifts reducing model accuracy over time.

Mitigation: Continuous Population Stability Index (PSI) tracking with automated retraining triggered whenever PSI exceeds 0.20.

Risk 4: Regulatory & Fair Lending Compliance

Threat: Algorithmic bias or non-discrimination regulatory scrutiny.

Mitigation: All demographic variables (`ethnic`, `marital`, `adults`) are strictly excluded from feature weighting. Scoring is 100% behavioral.
